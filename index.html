01 · The challenge

One scarcity.
Four linked crises.

People in Kibera struggle to obtain clean, affordable and reliable water. The shortage reaches beyond the tap, shaping health, education and household income.

01

Waterborne disease raises treatment costs and disrupts daily life.

02

Long collection journeys consume hours that could support learning or work.

03

Unsafe alternatives expose families to contamination and uncertainty.
